What each tool is

  • Reference: the Doyle–Fuller–Newman model with a lumped thermal model, solved with PyBaMM using the LG M50 parameters of O’Regan et al. (2022), cooled by 10 W/(m² K). It plays the real battery.
  • Simulation: a single-particle model with electrolyte (SPMe) and the same lumped thermal model, written from scratch in NumPy; it shares the reference’s parameters and is not fitted to any data.
  • Formula: Peukert’s law through two rated points (25 °C, new cell, 50 % and 100 % load).
  • Learned models: gradient boosting, a neural network and a Gaussian process (scikit-learn), trained on 150 noisy discharges inside the training range; physics features is a linear fit of log t on log load, log health and 1/T; simulation + data learns a correction to the simulation.

Why the cold room is not as bad as an isothermal model says

Limits

The reference is itself a model, not a measurement, and it shares its parameters with the simulation, which favours the simulation. The cooling coefficient of a cell inside a UPS cabinet is assumed, not measured. Ageing is loss of lithium plus an SEI film resistance only. The thermal-runaway model is a lumped 18650 cell without venting.
